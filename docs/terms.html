03Using the plugin

Use omastart on systems you own or are authorized to administer. Review the application and startup source before saving a change, and keep backups appropriate to your setup.

Changes affect startup configuration for future sessions. omastart does not start, stop, or restart applications. Disabling a globally enabled user service may create a per-user mask, which also blocks manual or indirect activation until restored. Unsupported or ambiguous configurations remain protected.

Undo and recovery depend on the saved record and the current file state. Edits made by other programs can prevent automatic restoration. Uninstalling omastart does not automatically undo startup changes or remove recovery backups.
